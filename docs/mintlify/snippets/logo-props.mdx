## Logo

The Fanvue brand logo. Supports full (icon + wordmark), icon-only, wordmark-only, and portrait (stacked) layouts with multiple colour schemes. `version="agencies"` renders the sub-brand lockup: a glossy purple icon and an "AGENCIES" label beneath the wordmark.

<ParamField path='aria-label' type='string'>
  Accessible label for the logo. Required when `variant` is `"icon"` and the logo is used inside interactive contexts (links, buttons). @example "Fanvue home"
</ParamField>

<ParamField path='color' type='"fullColour" | "decolour" | "whiteAlways" | "blackAlways"' default='fullColour'>
  Colour scheme of the logo.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path='size' type='"24" | "32" | "40" | "16" | "20" | "48" | "64"' default='32" (or "40" when `variant="icon"`)'>
  Height of the logo in pixels.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path='variant' type='"icon" | "full" | "wordmark" | "portrait"' default='full'>
  Layout variant of the logo.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path='version' type='"default" | "agencies"' default='default'>
  Sub-brand version of the logo.
</ParamField>
